## Attribution and notes

### Ghostbuster (essays, creative writing, news)

- Location: <https://github.com/vivek3141/ghostbuster-data>
- Licence: cc-by-3.0 (licence confirmed)
- Citation: Verma, Fleisig, Tomlin, Klein. "Ghostbuster: Detecting Text Ghostwritten by Large Language Models". NAACL 2024.
- Notes: Human essays (IvyPanda), r/WritingPrompts stories and Reuters 50-50 news, each with gpt-3.5-turbo and Claude (2023) counterparts written from the same prompt.

### ELLIPSE corpus (English-language-learner essays)

- Location: <https://github.com/scrosseye/ELLIPSE-Corpus>
- Licence: cc-by-nc-sa-4.0 (licence confirmed)
- Citation: Crossley, Tian, Baffour, Franklin, Benner, Boser. "A large-scale corpus for assessing written argumentation: PERSUADE 2.0" / "The English Language Learner Insight, Proficiency and Skills Evaluation (ELLIPSE) Corpus". IJLCR 2023.
- Notes: Non-native human writing, used as hard negatives so the detector does not learn "simple English = AI". Anonymisation placeholders (Generic_Name ...) are replaced with ordinary names during cleaning. Non-commercial licence.

### Beemo (expert- and LLM-edited model outputs)

- Location: <https://github.com/Toloka/beemo>

- Notes: Kept out of training on purpose: it is the main test of whether the detector still works after a person edits AI text.
